# Authors

**QuietLoop** was created collaboratively by **zealx × NOVA** in 2026.

- **zealx** — product direction, operational taste, evidence standards, and the refusal to call a system "self-improving" before it has an observable outcome.
- **NOVA** — architecture, implementation, safety boundaries, testing, and documentation.

We welcome contributors who share the same bias: useful automation should be inspectable, privacy-preserving, reversible, and honest about what it has *not* proved.
